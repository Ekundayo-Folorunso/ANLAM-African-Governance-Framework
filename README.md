# AFRICAN NATIONAL LEADERSHIP AND ACCOUNTABILITY MODEL (ANLAM)

### Rethinking Presidential Leadership, Accountability and Africa's Development Future

**A proposed African governance reform framework, using Nigeria as a pilot case.**

**Proponent:** Ekundayo Folorunso

**Status:** Independent policy proposal open to public discussion, constitutional examination and expert review.

---

## 1. About ANLAM

The African National Leadership and Accountability Model (ANLAM) is an independent governance reform proposal exploring a framework for presidential leadership assessment, accountability and national development.

The proposal is built around a fundamental question:

**How can African countries strengthen presidential leadership accountability while protecting democratic choice, constitutional institutions and the interests of their citizens?**

Using Nigeria as its pilot case, ANLAM proposes an institutional framework for assessing presidential leadership capacity, identifying national priorities and monitoring presidential performance against declared commitments.

## 2. The Central Proposal

At the heart of ANLAM is the proposed establishment of a **36-member Board of National Integrity and Presidential Accountability (BNIPA)**, with one representative from each of Nigeria's 36 states.

The proposed Board would:

- Assess national development priorities.
- Identify the leadership competencies required to address national challenges.
- Publish independent assessments of presidential candidates.
- Monitor presidential performance against declared commitments.
- Provide periodic public reports on national leadership accountability.

The Board would operate within defined constitutional boundaries and would not become a parallel government.

## 3. A Wider African Vision

ANLAM goes beyond presidential accountability.

Its wider ambition is to contribute to an Africa where strong institutions, accountable leadership and sustainable development create conditions for skilled people to remain, return, invest, innovate and prosper.

It recognises the importance of addressing the factors contributing to brain drain, including unemployment, weak infrastructure, insecurity, limited opportunities and inadequate institutional support.

**Our guiding vision:**

*We must build an Africa where our brightest minds do not have to leave to become their best.*

## 4. Core Principles

1. Presidential accountability.
2. Constitutional supremacy.
3. Democratic participation.
4. Institutional independence.
5. Integrity and professional competence.
6. Transparency and public scrutiny.
7. Continuity of national development priorities.
8. Human capital retention and sustainable development.

## 6. Invitation to National and Continental Debate

ANLAM is presented for independent examination, not automatic acceptance.

Constitutional lawyers, policymakers, political scientists, governance researchers, civil society organisations, journalists, professionals and interested members of the public are invited to examine the proposal.

Constructive criticism, alternative recommendations and evidence-based contributions are welcome.

The ultimate objective is to determine whether the proposed framework can be improved into a credible, constitutionally sound and democratically acceptable contribution to governance reform.

## 7. Important Notice

ANLAM is an independent proposal developed by Ekundayo Folorunso.

It is not an existing policy of the Nigerian Government, the African Union or any other government or institution.

Its recommendations are subject to independent expert review, constitutional scrutiny, public debate and any necessary legislative processes.
