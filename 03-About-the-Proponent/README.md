# About the Proponent

## Ekundayo Folorunso

**Proponent, African National Leadership and Accountability Model (ANLAM)**

Ekundayo Folorunso is the originator and proponent of the African National Leadership and Accountability Model (ANLAM), an independent policy proposal exploring presidential leadership assessment, accountability and national development.

His interest in the proposal is rooted in the conviction that Africa's development challenges require not only economic investment and institutional reform but also deliberate attention to the quality, accountability and long-term direction of national leadership.

Through ANLAM, he seeks to stimulate constructive national and continental discussions on presidential accountability, institutional independence, democratic participation and sustainable development.

The proposal uses Nigeria as its pilot case while exploring the possibility of adapting its underlying principles to other African countries, subject to their individual constitutional and political systems.

### Vision

To contribute to an Africa where accountable leadership, strong institutions and sustainable development create opportunities for people to stay, build, innovate and prosper.

### Commitment to Public Engagement

Ekundayo welcomes engagement with constitutional experts, policymakers, researchers, civil society organisations, journalists and members of the public.

ANLAM is presented as an independent proposal open to criticism, expert review, constructive debate and possible improvement.

### Guiding Statement

*We must build an Africa where our brightest minds do not have to leave to become their best.*
